StableLab · TrustLock Module

Payments held safely in trust

🔒 Confidential deal amounts
When two parties don't fully trust each other, TrustLock locks the money onchain instead of sending it directly to the other side, releasing it only when the agreed condition is met. The deal amount is encrypted: only the depositor and beneficiary see it by default. The arbiter stays neutral until access is explicitly granted, so a fair resolution path exists even in disputes.
Example scenario: You're about to pay a freelancer for a project. If you send the money directly, there's a risk they disappear before finishing; if they do the work first and wait for payment, the trust problem shifts to their side. With TrustLock, you lock the money and appoint a trusted third party as arbiter. The arbiter can't see how much is at stake until you or the beneficiary grants them access, usually only if a dispute actually arises.
