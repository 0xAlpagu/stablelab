VeloPay has been discontinued

During testing, we identified that the invoice creation flow did not verify or require the debtor's consent, which could allow the liquidity pool to be drained without a genuine underlying receivable. Rather than leave this live, we have taken the module down while we redesign it.

The remaining seven StableLab modules (Staking, Lending, Swap, Bridge, FlowPay, TrustLock, VaultGuard) are unaffected and fully functional.
