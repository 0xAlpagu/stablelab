Invoice factoring is a multi-billion-dollar global finance industry; it lets freelancers and businesses turn their term invoices into cash instantly. We built VeloPay for exactly this.

Example scenario: A freelancer does work for a global company and issues an invoice with 30-day terms. Instead of dealing with a cash flow crunch during that time, they upload their invoice to VeloPay and receive 97% of the amount in their wallet within seconds. When the due date arrives, the company pays directly into the pool; if late, a 0.1% daily late fee applies. Investors providing liquidity earn from this spread.
