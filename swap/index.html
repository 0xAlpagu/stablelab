StableLab · Swap Module

Instant swaps between two assets

Swap lets you exchange between USDC, EURC, and cirBTC instantly through decentralized pools. The price is calculated automatically based on the pool's ratios (the x·y=k formula); a small fee (0.3%) also lets liquidity providers earn.
Example scenario: You have USDC but need EURC for a euro-denominated payment (or you want exposure to cirBTC). Without going through an intermediary, you swap instantly, directly from the pool; the price is calculated transparently based on the pool's current ratio, and the transaction completes in seconds.
