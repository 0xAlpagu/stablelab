StableLab · Lending Module

Get liquidity without selling your asset

Lending lets you borrow USDC by posting USDC or cirBTC as collateral, without selling your assets. You can borrow instantly up to 66% of your collateral's value, and repay any time to withdraw it back.
Example scenario: You hold cirBTC but don't want to sell it (maybe you expect it to appreciate). You deposit it as collateral and borrow USDC against it; this way you keep your Bitcoin exposure while getting the liquidity you need. When you repay your debt, your collateral comes back to you.
01
Deposit collateral
You lock USDC or cirBTC into the contract as collateral.
02
Borrow
You borrow instantly up to 66% of your collateral.
03
Repay
You can close your debt whenever you like.
04
Withdraw collateral
If you have no debt, you get your collateral back.
