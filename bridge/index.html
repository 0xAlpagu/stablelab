StableLab · Bridge

Bring USDC to Arc from any chain

Bridge moves real USDC across chains using Circle's official Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP) — the same burn-and-mint mechanism Circle itself uses, not a wrapped token or a simulation. USDC is burned on the source chain and freshly minted on Arc, 1:1.
Example scenario: You hold USDC on Base Sepolia but want to use it inside StableLab's modules on Arc. Instead of manually bridging with a third-party service, you connect your wallet here, choose Base Sepolia as the source, and Bridge handles the approval, burn, attestation wait, and mint on Arc for you.
