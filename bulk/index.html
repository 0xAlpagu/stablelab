Pay many people in one transaction

🔒 Each recipient sees only their own amount
List every recipient and amount, and settle them all in a single onchain transaction. Each amount is encrypted separately: every recipient can only decrypt and see their own payment, never anyone else's on the same list.
Example scenario: It's payday for your 12-person team. Instead of sending 12 separate transactions, or one where everyone can see everyone else's pay, you send one encrypted batch. Each teammate can only decrypt their own amount; nobody sees what a colleague was paid.
